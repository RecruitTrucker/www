Our Commitment

We stand against human trafficking and child trafficking.

The trucking industry sits at a unique vantage point on America's highways. Recruit Trucker is built to keep the people who move freight safe — and to give them the tools to spot and report exploitation.

Zero tolerance. Recruit Trucker has no place for human trafficking, child trafficking, forced labor, or the exploitation of any person. Any account used to facilitate, advertise, or conceal these crimes is removed, and credible information is referred to the appropriate authorities.

Why this matters in trucking

Traffickers move victims along the same highways, truck stops, and rest areas that professional drivers use every day. That means drivers and carriers are often the people most likely to witness — and interrupt — trafficking in progress. We take that responsibility seriously, and we want every member of our community to know the signs and know how to act.

How our platform is designed to help

These are safeguards we actually build into the marketplace today — not promises for later:

Verified identities

Identity verification (via Stripe Identity) and manual review of professional credentials make it harder for bad actors to operate anonymously behind fake profiles.

Human moderation

Our staff can review, moderate, suspend, and remove accounts and listings. Postings and messages that violate our Terms are taken down.

Report tools

Every member can report a profile, listing, or message to our team through the in-app support system, and we follow up and track it to resolution.

Audit & accountability

Sensitive actions and staff access are logged, so we can cooperate with a lawful investigation when one is required.

Note: our safeguards deter and disrupt abuse; no platform can guarantee prevention. We continually improve these controls — see our roadmap for planned trust & safety enhancements.

Know the signs

Trafficking can be hard to see. Some indicators the anti-trafficking community asks drivers to watch for:

  • A person who appears to be under the control of someone else, or who isn't free to speak for themselves;
  • Someone who seems disoriented, fearful, or unable to say where they are or where they're going;
  • Minors traveling with unrelated adults under unclear circumstances, especially at truck stops or rest areas;
  • Vehicles dropping off or picking up people at odd hours around lots and rest areas;
  • Signs of physical abuse, malnourishment, or a lack of personal freedom or identification.

If a child is in immediate danger, call 911. Do not intervene physically or try to investigate yourself — your safety and the victim's safety come first.

How to report

If you see something, say something. Reporting is confidential and can save a life.

National Human Trafficking Hotline (U.S.) 1-888-373-7888

Text 233733 (BeFree) · Available 24/7, confidential, in many languages.

Missing or exploited children 1-800-843-5678

National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline: report.cybertip.org

Emergencies: if someone is in immediate danger, call 911. To report abuse of Recruit Trucker itself, contact us at support@recruittrucker.com or use the in-app report tools — and please contact law enforcement first if a person is at risk.
