Privacy Policy

EFFECTIVE DATE: 2026-07-09  ·  LAST UPDATED: 2026-07-09

This Privacy Policy describes how UrdaSoft, LLC doing business as Recruit Trucker ("Recruit Trucker," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use Recruit Trucker (the "Service") at recruittrucker.com and app.recruittrucker.com. By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide to us

  • Account and profile data. Your email, password (stored hashed), display name, role (driver, company, recruiter, or broker), headline, location, and the role-specific profile fields you provide.
  • Credential documents. Files you upload for verification (such as a CDL or DOT/MC authority), which are stored in access-controlled (private) storage.
  • User content. Job and load postings, applications and bids, messages, and reviews you create.
  • Communications. If you contact us (for example, at support@recruittrucker.com), we collect the contents of your message and any information you include.

Information from identity verification

  • Identity verification data. Identity verification is performed by Stripe Identity, which processes a government-issued ID and a selfie. We do not receive or store your ID images or biometric data — we store only the verification result/status and a Stripe session reference.
  • Payment data. If you purchase a paid subscription, payment is processed by Stripe; we do not store full card numbers.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage, device, and security data. Analytics (via Google Analytics 4), IP address, device and browser type, and security/audit logs — including logged admin/staff access to personal information.
  • Cookies and similar technologies. Used to distinguish visitors and measure usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate the marketplace — accounts, profiles, listings, messaging, and reviews;
  • To perform and record identity and credential verification;
  • To enforce your per-field privacy visibility settings and the proxy-contact system;
  • To respond to inquiries and provide support;
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and rate-limit violations;
  • To process subscriptions (when you purchase them);
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in the following circumstances:

  • With other users, per your visibility settings. The platform enforces a per-field visibility model (private / connections / public, default private). Your real name, email, phone, and exact location are governed by your settings and the viewer's relationship to you, and contact may occur through a proxy email relay until you choose to share your real details.
  • Service providers (processors). Stripe (identity verification; payments when you subscribe), Supabase (database and file-storage hosting), and Google Analytics, which process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.
  • Business transfers. In a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction, with notice.

4. Data Retention

Account and profile data is retained while your account is active. Credential documents and rejected verification submissions are retained for defined windows and purged on expiry or account deletion, subject to legal-hold exceptions. Message content and audit logs have their own retention windows. Analytics data is retained according to our Google Analytics configuration.

5. Identity Verification and Biometrics

Identity verification uses Stripe Identity, which captures and processes a government-issued ID and a selfie (which may involve biometric analysis) under Stripe's terms and privacy policy. You consent to this processing before verification begins; we receive only the outcome and a session reference, not your ID images.

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Visibility and proxy. Control the per-field visibility of your profile and use a proxy display name and email relay.
  • Access, correction, deletion, export. Request access to, correction of, export of, or deletion of your data by emailing support@recruittrucker.com. Deletion revokes your sessions and honors retention and legal-hold exceptions.
  • Cookies. Most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies. You can also opt out of Google Analytics using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

California residents: Under the CCPA/CPRA, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, request correction or deletion, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To exercise your rights, contact support@recruittrucker.com.

7. Data Security

We use access-controlled storage with row-level security, server-side redaction enforced at the API boundary, private storage for credential documents, audited admin/staff access to personal information, encrypted transmission (HTTPS), and rate limiting on sensitive endpoints. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service requires users to be 18 or older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. International Visitors

The Service is operated from the United States. If you access it from outside the United States, you understand that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those of your jurisdiction.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy on this page and revise the "Last Updated" date above. Material changes may also be communicated in-app or by email.
