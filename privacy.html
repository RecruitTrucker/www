This Privacy Policy describes how UrdaSoft, LLC doing business as Recruit Trucker ("Recruit Trucker," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you visit recruittrucker.com (the "Site"), join our waitlist, or otherwise interact with us. By using the Site, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide to us

  • Email address. When you join our waitlist, we collect the email address you submit so we can notify you when the platform launches and share related updates.
  • Communications. If you contact us (for example, at support@recruittrucker.com), we collect the contents of your message and any information you choose to include.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage and analytics data. We use Google Analytics 4 to collect information about how visitors use the Site, including pages viewed, buttons clicked, scroll depth, approximate location (city/region level), device and browser type, and referring pages.
  • Cookies and similar technologies. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar identifiers to distinguish visitors and measure Site usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To notify you when Recruit Trucker launches and provide early access to the platform;
  • To send you updates about the platform, features, and related news (you can unsubscribe at any time);
  • To understand how visitors use the Site so we can improve it;
  • To respond to your inquiries and provide support;
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues, abuse, or fraud;
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers. We use trusted third-party providers to operate the Site, including Supabase (database hosting for waitlist entries) and Google Analytics (site analytics). These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.
  • Business transfers. If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you of any change in ownership or use of your personal information.

4. Data Retention

We retain waitlist email addresses until the earlier of (a) your request to be removed, or (b) the point at which the information is no longer needed for the purposes described above. Analytics data is retained according to our Google Analytics configuration.

5. Your Rights and Choices

  • Unsubscribe. You may opt out of our emails at any time by using the unsubscribe link in any message or by contacting us.
  • Access, correction, deletion. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you by emailing support@recruittrucker.com.
  • Cookies. Most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies. You can also opt out of Google Analytics using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Do Not Track. The Site does not currently respond to Do Not Track signals.

California residents: Under the CCPA/CPRA, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To exercise your rights, contact support@recruittrucker.com.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted transmission (HTTPS) and access-controlled storage. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. International Visitors
