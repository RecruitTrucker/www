How it works

Recruit Trucker is a marketplace — brokers and carriers post freight, and verified carriers and owner-operators bid on it.

Carriers Find & win loads

Find RV and oversize vehicle loads for your equipment, bid your rate, and coordinate any oversize routing directly with the broker or dealer.

Brokers Post & award freight

Post the RV class, dimensions, and whether it's driveaway or towed, then award the verified carrier with the right equipment and rating.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a dedicated RV category?

Not yet — post RVs under the vehicle category and note the class and dimensions (a dedicated RV category is on our roadmap). Carriers factor any oversize/overheight routing into their bid.

Driveaway or towed?

Both — specify on the load so carriers with the right method and equipment can bid.

Who can post RV loads?

Verified dealers, auctions, and brokers with a broker or carrier profile.
